The two-thirds rule and the marketing around it

AHAM's honest rule: CADR at least two-thirds of the room's square footage gives about 4.8 air changes an hour - enough to matter. The "covers 500 sq ft" on the box assumes the same unit at 1.5 air changes an hour, which moves more marketing than air. Smoke is the smallest common pollutant, so the smoke CADR is the number that tells the truth; pollen is easy and dust is in between. And a big unit on a medium setting beats a small unit screaming at max - same clean air, half the noise.
